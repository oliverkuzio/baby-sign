First production release of Baby Sign.

- **Dictionary, phrasebook and songbook** — browse signs for words, phrases and songs, with tag filtering and pagination, and see the phrases and songs that use each word.
- **Song videos** to sign along to.
- **Favourites** for words, phrases and songs, collected on your dashboard.
- **GitHub sign-in**, with admin-only tools for creating, editing and deleting content.
- **Light and dark themes**, a mobile navigation dock, and an **installable PWA** that keeps working offline for pages you've already visited.
